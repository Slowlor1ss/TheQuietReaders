## Introduction

Published in December 2023 by Brynne Weaver, this dark romance follows a unique mix of comedy and spice that made readers fall in love with her writing. *Butcher and Blackbird* is the first book in the Ruinous Love Trilogy.

---

## A Summary

Rowan knows he likes Sloane when he finds her in a cage… Next to a dead body… And tons of maggots. He invites her to join him in a yearly game stalking monsters across the country, and this leads to a mutual friendship between two messed-up people trying to make the world better. As the years go on, the friendship develops into something more, they have to reckon with their past in order to move forward.


## Writing Style

The writing style is heavily focused on comedic value mixed with horny themes, which works better than you can imagine. The book has two POVs - Sloane and Rowan - but all POVs are in first person, so there are a lot of internal monologues (especially because of Sloane’s anxiety). The chapter lengths vary massively, but it is such a fast read overall.


## My Thoughts

*Butcher and Blackbird* is a book I knew I would like if it was done correctly, and truly it was. I read this book as an immersive read (I listened to it and read it at the same time) and that just made the comedy hit much harder. I had a smile on my face the entire time… well maybe apart from the cookie dough ice cream scene. I also loved the fact that they were more of a vigilante type serial killer, because I was very concerned that it would be idolizing the murders of innocent people. Luckily, every person murdered in the novel was a stupid, disgusting person.  
Sloane and Rowan were both amazing characters and I loved the ‘he fell first’ trope for this story. It felt fresh and matched with his personality. The way he created a game in order to keep her around, the way he sent food to her house to teach her to cook and the way he integrated her into his life as a whole was the highlight of the book. I love a man who loves his girlie and is not afraid to show it. 

I also really loved the plot twist, despite the fact I did see it coming. I think it worked and was what the last part of the novel needed to keep some momentum before the full resolution.  
I also loved the side characters - each had so much personality and their own lives that intermingle with one another perfectly. I cannot wait to read the next book from Lark’s perspective.
