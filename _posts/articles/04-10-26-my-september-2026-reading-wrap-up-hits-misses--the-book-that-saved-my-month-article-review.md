<h2>My September 2026 Reading Wrap Up</h2><p>September was a struggle for everyone I know, and I couldn’t escape the same fate. Out of the 9 books I read this month, I didn’t manage to hit a single 5 star read (although I did get close with one satirical fiction) nor did most of the books I finished even reach a 4 star. <br/>Consider this a friendly warning, and a gossip session about what I truly dislike in writing, as well as a few treasures in the mud mixed in. Here are the 9 books I read in September 2026.</p><p></p><h2>Startup Hell by Caitlin Rozakis</h2><p>
<img src="/assets/images/articles/inline/startup-hell-by-caitlin-rozakis-100705.webp" alt="startup-hell-by-caitlin-rozakis" loading="lazy" style="max-width: 350px; width: 35vw; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; margin: 20px auto; display: block;">
</p><p><em>Publish Date - 19 May 2026</em></p><p><em>Rating: 2/5</em></p><p>I wanted to start my month with something to get me in the cosy but spooky feeling, and this truly feels like it should be the best pick for it. It has romance, demons, a bad-ass mother and an MC that just wants to live her life. However, I didn’t realise quite how much of corporate America would be contained in these pages. I, personally, have never worked in sales or marketing, but I would consider myself an expert after reading this book because there were so many explanations and discussions about them. It took me out of the cosiness and sent me straight into work mode. </p><p>I think you’ll like this book if you work in that area, because a lot of the humour will hit harder and it won’t rip you out of your cosiness.</p><p></p><p><em>Synopsis:</em></p><p><em>Morgan Blackwater's mother is a kickass, world-saving, demon-slaying Shadow Council wizard.
