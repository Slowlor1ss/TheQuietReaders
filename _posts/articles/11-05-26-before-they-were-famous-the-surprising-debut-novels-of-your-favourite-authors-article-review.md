<h2>Before They Were Famous: The Surprising Debut Novels of Your Favourite Authors </h2><p></p><p>Every iconic author has a starting point, and honestly, some of those beginnings are wild. From Frieda McFadden to Emily Henry, many of today’s most beloved writers launched their careers with books that are underrated, surprising, or quietly brilliant. If you’re curious about where your favourites began, here’s a tour through the debut novels that set the stage for their success. </p><p></p><h2>The Love Hypothesis by Ali Hazelwood</h2><p>
<img src="/assets/images/articles/inline/the-love-hypothesis-by-ali-hazelwood-191752.webp" alt="the-love-hypothesis-by-ali-hazelwood" loading="lazy" style="max-width: 350px; width: 35vw; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; margin: 20px auto; display: block;">
</p><p>Publish Date - 21st October 2021</p><p></p><p>It’s hard to believe that this was Ali Hazelwood’s debut novel, with how quickly it was ingrained into the romance genre forever. Her flawless mix of STEM wrapped in spicy romance filled a gap in the fiction community that became truly beloved, and this love has only grown as Hazelwood has put out similar novels over the years. </p><p>Now that it is set to become a movie, fans are re-reading this debut novel as a form of recapturing nostalgia, and reigniting their years-old love for her writing.</p><p>This was one of the first romance novels that I ever read, and I immediately fell in love with her writing style, and her unique ability to make me smile at the page. </p><p>Publish Date -23rd August 2013</p><p></p><p>Frieda McFadden has undoubtedly become one of the most staple authors in modern fiction, with the likes of <em>The Housemaid</em> being a fan favourite. The Devil Wears Scrubs allows readers to see a completely different side of McFadden, as it markets itself as a medical humour novel. You follow Jane McGill as she goes through her first month of her internship at County Hospital. With a 4.0 Amazon rating, readers clearly enjoyed this lighter, more comedic side of McFadden. </p><p>Publish Date -18th November 2013</p><p></p><p>Tessa Bailey has always written steamy romances that are loved by many romance readers across the world, but her first novel isn’t as well known as her later works. <em>Protecting What’s His</em> is book 1 in the <em>‘A Line of Duty’</em> series, her first series ever produced, with each book surrounding police officers. <img src="/assets/images/articles/inline/the-love-that-split-the-world-by-emily-henry-191810.webp" alt="the-love-that-split-the-world-by-emily-henry" loading="lazy" style="max-width: 350px; width: 35vw; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; margin: 20px auto; display: block;">
</p><p>Publish Date -2th January 2016</p><p></p><p>Emily Henry is one of the most notable names, tackling themes of grief and healing within her romance novels. With her new release - <em>Great Big Beautiful Life</em> - it may be time to look back at where this phenomenal writer started. <em>The Love That Split The World</em> is described as a YA romance, similar to her most recent novels, but many have described her debut as especially heart-breaking to read. The main difference that may excite you is the introduction of magic inside her debut, which intrigued me beyond belief. It’s no doubt that her writing is unlike any other, but the sprinkle of magic within her debut just adds an extra spark that I, personally, adored while reading it.</p><p></p><div class="affiliate-section">Amazon</div><p></p><hr/><p></p><p></p><h2>The Friend Zone by Abby Jimenez</h2><p>
