<h2><strong>Daisy Pearce Books in Order: The Dark Psychological Thrillers Everyone Is Sleeping On</strong></h2><p></p><p>Born in Cornwall, Daisy Pearce became obsessed with horror at far too young of an age and has been fascinated ever since. She remembers reading ‘Cujo’ by Stephen King when she was younger, and he has grown to be one of her influences. Over the years, Shirley Jackson and Gillian Flynn have been added to that list of influences within her writing. <br/>She began writing short stories as a teenager, with the first one called ‘The Black Prince’ published in One Eye Grey Magazine. Another one of her short stories was performed on stage at the Small Story Cabaret in Lewes in 2016. <br/>In 2015, her first full-length novel won a bursary with The Literary Consultancy, and later that year Pearce won the Chindi Authors Competition with another short story titled ‘Worm Food’.
