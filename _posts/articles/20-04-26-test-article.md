<h2 style="text-align: center">The Shippers by Katherine Center</h2>

JoJo Burton has spent her whole life being spectacularly bad at love, so she decides her sister’s cruise‑ship wedding is the perfect place to finally sort herself out. Her longtime crush, the guy who gave her her first kiss and is now conveniently newly divorced, will be on board, and she’s convinced that flirting with him for a week will magically fix her commitment issues. Since her sister is busy doing bridal things, JoJo drags her childhood best friend, Cooper, into the plan. Cooper is the man who said he wasn’t coming and then showed up anyway, the man who moved to London without a goodbye, the man who basically cracked her heart like a dropped iPhone. Seeing him again feels amazing and awful at the same time, and the more they hang out, the more she keeps thinking about all the things she never asked him.

<h2 style="text-align: center">First and Forever  by Lynn Painter</h2>

Duffy has her priorities straight: her dad, her cat and Minneapolis Coyotes football. So when she accidentally becomes the internet villain of the week for shoving the team mascot after he got a little too flirty, she’s horrified. To fix the whole mess, she agrees to a live interview, only to find out she’ll be sharing the screen with Connor Cunningham, the team’s golden boy. Connor thought he was just popping on to read a prepared statement, not get into a debate with a fan, but their back‑and‑forth ends up going viral in the best way. His PR team immediately sees an opportunity and tells him to ask Duffy out. The only rule is that she can’t know it’s all for show. Once they start spending time together, the fake dating starts to feel a lot less fake, and Connor realises he can’t keep the truth hidden forever. If he messes this up, he won’t just lose a PR stunt. He’ll lose her.

<h2 style="text-align: center">
    Harvest Season by Brynne Weaver</h2>

The second book in the Seasons of Carnage trilogy dives back into Cape Carnage just as its secrets start pushing to the surface. Nolan Rhodes is trying to make sense of a town that keeps twisting beneath his feet. Every truth he uncovers only leads to something darker. Harper isn’t the woman he thought she was, Arthur’s intentions feel more dangerous by the day, and Sheriff Yates seems to be watching from every corner. When true crime obsessives flood the town searching for answers about their missing leader, Nolan is forced into a search and rescue mission he knows is already hopeless. At the same time, Harper is struggling to hold herself together now that her past has been dragged into the light. She doesn’t know who to trust, not the man she’s falling for, not the mentor she’s sworn to protect, and not even herself. Loyalties strain, relationships fracture, and love becomes its own kind of threat.
