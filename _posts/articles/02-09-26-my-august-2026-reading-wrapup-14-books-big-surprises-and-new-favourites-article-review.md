<h2>My August 2026 Reading Wrap Up</h2><p>This month, I have finished 14 books, from YA mysteries to horror to literary fiction. There’s a wide range of novels - some that you may not have heard of before, so here is a collection of them, with some of my thoughts and recommendations.</p><p></p><h2>The Hawthorne Legacy by Jennifer Lynn Barnes</h2><p>
<img src="/assets/images/articles/inline/the-hawthorne-legacy-by-jennifer-lynn-barnes-111327.webp" alt="the-hawthorne-legacy-by-jennifer-lynn-barnes" loading="lazy" style="max-width: 350px; width: 35vw; height: auto; border-radius: 8px; margin: 20px auto; display: block;">
</p><p><em>Publish Date - 07 September 2021</em></p><p><em>Rating: 4/5</em></p><p>The second book in the Inheritance Games series, it truly is a quick and easy read.
