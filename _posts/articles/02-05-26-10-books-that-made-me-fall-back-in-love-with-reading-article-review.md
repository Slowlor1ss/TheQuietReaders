<h2>10 Books That Made Me Fall Back In Love With Reading</h2><p></p><p>Reading is ultimately a hobby, and it’s one that can get exhausting. You are constantly putting yourself into different worlds, lives, minds, and experiences, which can be hauntingly beautiful and tiring. </p><p>I read this book just when I needed it. It provided depth that a lot of other books just don’t. It spoke of death, grief, and times when love isn’t enough by itself, which rings true to plenty of people’s real lives.
